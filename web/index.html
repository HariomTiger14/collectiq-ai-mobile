Questions

Before you ask.

Does PackLox ever make up a price?

No. If there isn't strong enough provider evidence for an item, PackLox shows an honest "unavailable" state instead of guessing — this is a hard rule across every category, not a fallback.

What does the AI actually do, if not set the price?

AI identifies what you scanned — title, set, card number, condition clues. It never invents a value. Pricing always comes from a connected provider's real market data.

What happens to my photos and data?

Saved records belong to your account. You can request a full export or permanent deletion of your data at any time.

Which categories have live pricing today?

All 12 categories on the Home screen have real, provider-backed pricing today — Pokémon, MTG, Yu-Gi-Oh, One Piece, and sports cards, video games, comics, LEGO, Funko Pop, coins, and sneakers/streetwear — with more added as trusted providers connect.
